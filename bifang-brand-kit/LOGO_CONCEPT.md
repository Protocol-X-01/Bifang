# Bifang Logo Concept

## Direction

Combine three ideas:

1. The one-legged Bifang warning bird
2. Controlled fire as accelerated computation and transformation
3. A terminal cursor as developer control and forward motion

The identity should feel like a serious open-source developer tool, not a fantasy game, esports team, or generic chatbot.

## Symbol

Use a geometric crane-like bird in a rising posture with one visible supporting leg, angular wings suggesting code brackets or terminal panes, a simple head and beak, negative-space cursor or chevron, and a restrained circular motion stroke. Avoid crowns, phoenix ornament, excessive flames, and pseudo-Chinese styling.

## Wordmark

```text
BIFANG
Build. Run. Verify.
```

Use an uppercase geometric sans-serif with wide tracking, squared terminals, and excellent small-size legibility.

## Palette

```text
Bifang Red     #E63946
Flame Orange   #FF8A3D
Cloud White    #F7F7F7
Deep Slate     #0F172A
Success Green  #22C55E
```

Success Green is reserved for verified outcomes, not the core logo.

## Required variants

- Full-colour horizontal lockups for dark and light backgrounds
- Square icon
- One-colour white and black marks
- Simplified 16-pixel favicon
- Terminal-safe monochrome symbol
- Social preview with wordmark and tagline

## Rules

- Maintain accessible contrast
- Pair status colours with labels and icons
- Do not stretch, bevel, rotate, or shadow the mark
- Do not insert NVIDIA or Nebius logos into the Bifang mark
- Do not portray Bifang as a Western phoenix
- Keep third-party attribution separate

## Voice

Precise, calm, evidence-driven, technically confident, and transparent about risk. Avoid claims such as magical, omniscient, fully safe, or replacing engineers.
