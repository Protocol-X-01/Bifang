# Using the Bifang identity

Use the ember mark with white lettering on dark surfaces, or the ember mark with slate lettering on light surfaces. White and black variants are ready for single-colour work. Every logo has a transparent canvas; avatar tiles and promotional artwork have deliberate backgrounds.

Keep the mark's proportions and its single supporting leg intact. Leave at least one body-width of empty space between the visible symbol and neighbouring content. Avoid stretching, rotating, adding bevels or placing it over a busy image. The empty margins inside each exported symbol are intentional.

Use the full symbol at 64 pixels or larger. At 16–48 pixels use icons/favicon.svg or its dedicated PNG exports. Prefer horizontal lockups at 360 pixels or wider so the tagline remains readable. Use the symbol alone if the available space is smaller.

## Promotion

The three thumbnail templates are 1920 × 1080. **Coding demo** is the default: it pairs the current desktop capture with Build. Run. Verify. **Reviewed patch** refers to the five original checks visible in the retained demo task. **Brand** is a reusable introduction with the generated ember background. Match any thumbnail's wording to the video it introduces.

Social exports include 1200 × 630, 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1920 and 1584 × 396 layouts. The YouTube banner is 2560 × 1440, with important text in the centred area. A separate crop-guide image is included for checking placement; upload the clean banner. YouTube currently recommends 2560 × 1440 with a 6 MB maximum; confirm the platform preview before saving. [YouTube channel branding guidance](https://support.google.com/youtubecreatorstudio/answer/10456525?hl=en)

For native app icons and phone/tablet artwork, follow [Apple assets](APPLE_ASSETS.md) or [Android assets](ANDROID_ASSETS.md). The mobile brand board groups these exports with the Companion, Terminal and IDE family lockups.

## Editing and exporting

Start from the SVGs, or duplicate source/build_brand_pack.py and change its copy, layout values and palette. Captures and the generated background are embedded in exported SVGs, so they remain portable. Keep source artwork and font credits with a shared pack.

For screen use, choose PNG or SVG. For print, give the printer an SVG master and ask for a proof in their required colour profile. The supplied PNGs are screen artwork; no press-specific conversion is claimed.
